Welcome! This is the new home for news from the **Dharmamitra Project**. We'll
use this space to share research updates, model and tool releases, write-ups of
what we're working on, and the occasional deeper dive into the methods behind
our work on Buddhist and classical Asian languages.

## Why a blog?

Our [main site]({{ site.main_site }}) is where the tools live — translation,
search, and OCR for Sanskrit, Tibetan, Chinese, Pāli, and more. This blog is the
place for the story *around* those tools: what's new, what's changing, and why.

## What to expect

- **Release notes** when we ship new features or models
- **Research notes** on the methods and datasets behind the project
- **Project updates** and collaborations with our partners

## How posts are written

Every post here is just a Markdown file. To add one, drop a file named
`YYYY-MM-DD-your-title.md` into the `_posts/` folder with a little front matter
at the top:

```markdown
---
title: "Your post title"
date: 2026-06-15
author: Your Name
tags: [release, research]
description: A one-line summary shown on the home page and in search results.
---

Your content here, in **Markdown**.
```

Push it to `main` and it publishes automatically. More on that in the
[README](https://github.com/dharmamitra/dharmamitra-blog#readme).

Stay tuned.
